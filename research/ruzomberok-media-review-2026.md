Research boundary: **3 October 2026**. Election date: **24 October 2026**. This review supplements the [original city research](ruzomberok-2026-audit.md). The additions are integrated into the local production dataset on branch `main`.

## Scope and resulting data

All **79 people** across the four registered contests were searched, including the seven shared regional-chair candidates. The [structured receipt](ruzomberok-media-review-2026.json) records **341 actual queries**, identity checks, exclusions, access attempts, all **82 baseline media/controversy claim IDs**, and **49 event-chain records**. Some chains cover different participants in the same story; 49 is not a count of independent disputes.

Each chain distinguishes the earliest recovered context, intermediate reports and responses, and the latest checked stage. A discovered article is not automatically the first publication about an event. Some receipts retain an initial discovery article in `origin`, with earlier recovered context in their chronology. Excluded later material is marked as excluded, rather than used to manufacture a conclusion. An unresolved ending remains unresolved as of the snapshot.

The integration adds **83 atomic claims** and **56 canonical sources**. Ružomberok now exposes **586 claims and 286 referenced sources**. The cycle contains 1,080 claims and the global register has 472 sources. Fourteen additions concern shared regional-chair people, so the Liptovský Mikuláš context also exposes them and now has 618 claims.

The roster remains 79 people and 100 candidacies: 4 mayor, 62 municipal council, 27 regional council and 7 regional chair. Candidate records, candidacies, districts, contests, snapshot configuration and campaign-finance records are unchanged. All pre-existing claim IDs remain.
